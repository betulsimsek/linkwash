# Linkwash Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 28 September 2026_

Linkwash does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **No network requests:** The extension never contacts any server, including its developer's servers.
- **Local processing:** URLs are processed only inside your browser, only to remove tracking parameters and unwrap redirect links.
- **Local settings:** The only data Linkwash stores is your settings: the on/off switch and the list of sites where you paused it. These stay in your browser's local extension storage and never leave your device.
- **No tracking:** There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, account or third-party code.

## Why Linkwash needs its permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Access to all websites (`<all_urls>`) and `declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess` | To remove tracking parameters from navigations on any site before the request is sent. |
| `webNavigation` | To unwrap redirect links (for example `google.com/url?q=…`) on top-level navigations. |
| `storage` | To remember your on/off setting and the sites where you paused Linkwash. |
| `contextMenus`, `clipboardWrite` | For the "Copy clean link" item in the right-click menu. |
